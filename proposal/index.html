The workFive pieces, one project

What we will build.

1

Every new lead lands itself

Website and info@ leads flow automatically into one clean, simple pipeline in your Streak, with the source recorded, and an instant warm acknowledgment goes back to the person the moment they reach out. Built with Mercedes, on the design she already started.

Jenn approves the acknowledgment wording before it ever sends, and no one is added to the marketing list without opting in.

Confirm before sendingVerify their Streak plan tier supports the Zapier connection (ask Mercedes), and where website form submissions deliver today. Adjust this card if either answer surprises.
2

The phone stops leaking

A tracked local number that rings Tria the way calls do now. Every call is recorded with AI notes saved to the lead's record, so nothing needs writing down twice. When a call is missed or comes in while Tria is on the line, the caller gets a same-minute text and email with her booking link, and a follow-up task appears in the pipeline so nobody vanishes.

Your published number is only forwarded, never moved. Reversible in minutes, and answering the phone does not change. Two good services can run this line; both are priced plainly in the table below with our recommendation, and we would start either on monthly billing, so nothing is locked in for a year before you have lived with it.

3

Booking without the calendar chore

Your existing Calendly, set up properly: one booking page limited to the windows Tria picks, nothing same-day, synced to her Google Calendar. Callers who could not get through schedule themselves. Tria maintains nothing. She just reads her day.

4

The paper stack, entered by us

The two and a half inches of lead sheets get scanned, read, checked by a person, and landed in the pipeline with everything we can identify tagged, newest first. The handful we cannot read gets settled in one short call with Tria.

5

Training and a one-page guide

A 45-minute walkthrough with Tria and Mercedes, recorded for Jenn, plus a plain-language guide: what happens automatically, what a person does, and who to call. Your team runs all of it without us.

Why nothing here is wasted. Every piece runs inside tools you already pay for. Whether Streak stays for good or gets replaced later, the phone line, the calendar, the captured leads, and the habits all carry straight over.

Deliberately not in this project

  • Cleaning up or migrating the Streak history. Tria purges the old leads first, at her own pace, and then we decide together whether Streak stays or gets replaced.
  • Email campaigns and segmentation. Jenn's October plans run exactly as she has them.
  • Dashboards and measurement.
  • Anything on the website.
  • Anything touching client documents. That waits for a written boundary from you, always.
  • Anything that screens callers away from a person.

Each of these is a later conversation, taken one at a time, the same way as this one.
